These are planning assumptions, not benchmarks. Engagement and conversion rates in particular vary enormously by market, offer and channel, and most published averages come from vendors selling the thing being measured. Treat them as a starting position. Replacing them with your own actual conversion data is the first thing worth doing, because a model built on your numbers is the one your board will accept.
