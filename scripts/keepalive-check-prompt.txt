{"source":"lead_capture","email":"keepalive-check@quotasuccess.com.au","company":"AUTOMATED KEEP-ALIVE CHECK - safe to ignore or delete","role":"Automated test","service_interest":"not_sure","sign_responses":[]}

If you get a 500, 502, a timeout, or anything else non-200, that almost always means the Supabase database has paused again -- say so explicitly and clearly as the headline finding, since that's the exact failure this routine exists to catch. Only delete rows matching that exact email -- never touch any other row in the leads table, and never touch any other table. Confirm the delete actually removed a row (do not assume success without checking).

If anything failed (site down, submission failed/timed out, the test row couldn't be deleted, or the database needed restoring), lead with that clearly, including the exact HTTP status or error you saw, since that's what needs action, and confirm whether you were able to self-heal it via restore.
